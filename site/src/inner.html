Other entitlement plays

rights · buybacks · OFS · NCDs · click a row for the plain-words breakdown
Rights issue — existing holders buy new shares cheap. You must own the stock on the record date. New shares dilute, so the TERP is the price after dilution: the real discount is price vs TERP, not vs market.
Buyback (tender) — the company buys shares back at a premium. Own it on the record date, tender in the window. Holdings under ₹2 lakh get a reserved 15% small-shareholder quota — far better acceptance odds.
OFS — promoter or government sells a block on the exchange at a floor price, usually a discount. Announced ~1 day ahead; the daily refresh watches for it.
NCD — a bond, not a stock. Judge on rating and coupon; there is no listing pop.
